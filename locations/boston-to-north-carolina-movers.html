How it works

What Happens Between Pickup And Delivery?

A long-distance relocation has several stages. Knowing what to expect helps you prepare your property and understand how your belongings will be handled.

  1. 01

    Discuss Your Move And Get An Estimate

    Start by sharing your current address, North Carolina destination, preferred date and an overview of the belongings you need moved.

    Tell us whether you need packing, furniture disassembly, storage or other help. These details let our team understand the scope of the move and prepare a suitable estimate.

  2. 02

    Get Your Belongings Ready To Travel

    Before loading, household furniture and other items should be prepared according to their size, condition and handling needs.

    This may mean wrapping furniture in moving blankets, protecting fragile items, boxing up smaller belongings and taking apart suitable furniture.

  3. 03

    Loading And Transport

    Once preparation is done, belongings are loaded into the truck and arranged for the trip.

    Careful positioning and securing matter on a long-distance journey. The pickup schedule and route depend on your addresses, moving date and transport arrangements.

  4. 04

    Arrival At Your North Carolina Property

    When the shipment reaches its destination, belongings are unloaded and brought into the property according to the agreed arrangements.

    Furniture can be reassembled where included in your service, and larger items can be placed in their intended rooms.

  5. 05

    Final Placement And Removal Of Moving Materials

    After unloading, you can check that your belongings have been delivered and placed as discussed.

    The agreed service may also include removing moving-related packing materials and post-move cleanup. This covers moving debris and packing materials, not a full professional cleaning service.

Your Boston To North Carolina Moving Questions Answered

Will my furniture be protected during the journey?

Furniture protection can include moving blankets and protective plastic to help reduce the risk of scratches and surface damage during handling and transport.

Can you provide packing materials and help with packing?

Packing help and materials can be discussed when you arrange your move. Options may include boxes, tape, bubble wrap, moving blankets and protective wrapping.

Are there items that can't be transported?

Some hazardous, flammable, perishable or otherwise restricted items may not be suitable for transport in a moving truck. Requirements vary, so ask our team about any unusual or possibly restricted belongings before packing them.

Do you offer commercial moving to North Carolina?

Can you store my belongings if my new home isn't ready?
