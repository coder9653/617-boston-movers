Before you move

Things To Consider Before Moving To Pennsylvania

Relocating to another state is a good chance to plan ahead. Alongside arranging your moving service, think about the practical details that may affect your move and your first few weeks in Pennsylvania.

Set A Realistic Moving Schedule

Your moving timeline depends on the size of your property, the distance to your destination, the services required and the agreed delivery arrangements. Confirm your preferred dates early and allow enough time for packing, preparation and access arrangements.

Compare Living Costs In Your Destination

Living costs can vary a lot across Pennsylvania. Housing, utilities, transport and everyday expenses may differ between Philadelphia, Pittsburgh, smaller cities and rural communities. Research the area where you plan to settle so you can set a suitable budget.

Prepare For Seasonal Weather

Pennsylvania has distinct seasons, including cold winters, snow in some areas and warm summers. Weather can affect travel conditions and moving arrangements, especially in winter. Check the forecast before moving day and keep essential belongings within reach.

How are moving costs from Boston to Pennsylvania calculated?

How long does a move from Boston to Pennsylvania take?

Do you provide packing materials for an interstate move?

Can you move furniture from a Boston apartment to a Pennsylvania house?

Can you disassemble and reassemble furniture?
