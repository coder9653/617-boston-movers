Before moving day

Planning Your Relocation: Details To Check Before Moving Day

A move between Boston and Maryland can involve different property layouts, building rules and access arrangements. Handling a few practical details early can make pickup and delivery much more straightforward.

Check Building Access And Parking

Some Boston properties have narrow stairs, shared entrances, limited street parking or set moving hours. Maryland apartments and residential communities may have their own rules for delivery vehicles.

Ask your current and new property managers about access, parking, loading areas and any reservations you need.

Sort Your Belongings Before Packing

Take time to decide which furniture, appliances, boxes and personal items you want to bring. An organised inventory helps estimate the shipment size and makes it easier to discuss the right arrangements.

Set aside anything you plan to donate, sell or throw away before moving day.

Flag Fragile And Oversized Items

Artwork, mirrors, glass furniture, large appliances and delicate belongings may need extra preparation. Mention these items when you request your estimate so their handling can be planned in advance.

Coordinate Your Property Dates

If you are moving into a rented or purchased home, confirm when you can access the new address. Your moving plans should take into account lease dates, closing schedules, key handover and any limits on delivery times.

Keep Essentials Within Reach

Keep important documents, daily medication, valuables, chargers and essential personal items separate from the main shipment where it makes sense. Having them handy can make your first few days in the new home easier.
